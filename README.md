# Mood Garden

## Project Overview

Mood Garden is an interactive mood check-in website that turns emotions into a small digital garden. Users can reflect on how they feel by adjusting five mood sliders: Joy, Calm, Anxiety, Sadness, and Hope.

Based on the selected mood levels, the website generates a garden of flowers. Each flower represents part of the user's emotional state, creating a visual and interactive way to explore feelings.

The project is designed as a creative self-reflection experience rather than a mental health assessment tool.

## Key Features

**Mood Check-in:** Adjust five sliders to describe your current emotional state.
**Dynamic Flower Generation:** Generate a garden based on the proportions of different emotions.
**Interactive Flowers:** Select individual flowers to explore and interact with them.
**Garden Care:** Use Water, Feed, and Sunlight actions to care for the garden.
**Reflection Prompt:** Open a reflection prompt to think more about your current feelings.
**Visual Animation:** Enjoy gentle flower movements and atmospheric effects.
**Responsive Layout:** Use the website across different screen sizes.

## How to Use

1. Open the Mood Garden website.
2. Adjust the five mood sliders to reflect how you feel.
3. Click **Grow** to generate your garden.
4. Select a flower to interact with it.
5. Use **Water**, **Feed**, and **Sunlight** to care for your garden.
6. Use the reflection feature to spend a moment thinking about your emotions.
7. Click **Reset** if you want to start a new check-in.

## Design Concept

The garden acts as a visual metaphor for emotional growth. Different emotions are represented through flowers and their changing proportions. Rather than treating emotions as something that must be corrected, Mood Garden encourages users to acknowledge their feelings and give themselves time and space to reflect.

## Development Process

The project went through nine iterations, with changes to its visual direction, interface structure, flower generation, animation, and interaction details. The development process focused on gradually improving the relationship between the visual experience and the user's emotional reflection.

For more details, please refer to [PROCESS.md](PROCESS.md).
